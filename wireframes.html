02 / Structure

Four layers

Each layer answers one visitor completely before offering the next. Nobody is ever asked to scroll through material meant for someone else.

L1
Overview
The hiring manager. Name, position, three works, one line about now. Under a minute, no scrolling required to get the point.
L2
Selected work & career
The creative director. Seven projects and a career spine. Enough to judge taste and level without commitment.
L3
Case dossiers
The studio lead. Challenge, approach, precise role, credits, process, outcome, gallery. Depth on demand only.
L4
Archive
The researcher, and anyone checking you out. Every project and client, filterable. Where scale lives.

Progressive disclosure is the whole design. Volume is the enemy of seniority — but hidden volume, available on request, is proof of it.

06 / Direction

Visual direction — Index & Dossier

Editorial and disciplined. A grotesque carries structure; a serif appears only for premises and quoted lines; a monospace handles labels, credits and data. Generous margins, hairline rules, a numbered index logic borrowed from a production company's job list.

Images sit in framed plates at deliberate scale, never stretched full-bleed. This is a decision made from a constraint: much of what you hold exports at 250–640 px. Presenting images at considered size, inside a frame, reads as art direction. Blowing them up reads as a broken portfolio.

Motion is restrained — opacity and an 8–12 px rise, 300–400 ms, and nothing that moves unless a person asked it to. prefers-reduced-motion honoured throughout.

Full reset from the 2025 portfolio. The electric blue gradient, the yellow accent and the lowercase manifesto are the opposite of this. Nothing carries over except the facts.
